# Strömbron physical deck profile repair

The independently estimated public and PSV carriageways share both source bank heights but had a 3.7 m interior profile disagreement. The existing same-name, same-layer, parallel-geometry grouping now allows that case only when both matched bank heights agree within 15 cm and interior disagreement is below 4 m. Different layers and independent bank heights remain separate.

Exact native-source stall and held-guide floor queries are retained as RED/GREEN focused exports. The composed fixture produces usable normals and floor heights matching the unchanged upper source road profile. The complete city grouping audit changes only the three Strömbron source ways. These focused results alone do not establish full-course driving acceptance. Full root export and subsequent native tests are recorded separately.
